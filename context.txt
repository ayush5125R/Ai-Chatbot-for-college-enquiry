### **About IILM University**
IILM University operates campuses in locations such as Gurugram and Greater Noida, offering various undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs.

### **Admissions Process**
The admission process can be completed online or through a walk-in visit:
1. Registration: Register on the official website (iilm.edu) to receive an application link.
2. Application: Complete the online form with details and statement of purpose.
3. Documentation: Upload 10th and 12th-grade mark sheets, and photograph.
4. Selection: Based on academic achievements, extracurriculars, statement of purpose, and sometimes national entrance exams (CUET, JEE, CAT, XAT, CMAT). Shortlisted candidates are invited for a personal interview.
5. Final Admission: Successful candidates are enrolled.
